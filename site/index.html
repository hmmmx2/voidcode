ML & systems interview preparation

An editor that runs the whole thing on your machine.

VoidCode is a desktop editor for machine-learning interview preparation. Problems, projects and mock interviews, with a grader that runs your Python locally, and a tutor you can point at a model on your own hardware.

Free and open source, under the Apache License 2.0.

Nothing leaves your computer by default

Your code, your solutions and your conversations are stored in one file on your own disk. There is no telemetry, and an account is optional — see the Privacy Policy.

Your Python, graded locally

Submissions run inside the app in a WebAssembly Python sandbox with no network access, so a problem set works on a plane and a laptop with no GPU is enough.

Bring your own model, or ours

Point the tutor at Ollama or llama.cpp on your own machine, add an OpenRouter key, or sign in and use the hosted VoidCode model, which is metered by the second it spends answering.

Which Mac do I have?

Apple menu › About This Mac. A line reading Chip: Apple M1 (or M2, M3, M4) means Apple silicon — take the arm64 download. A line reading Processor: Intel means the x64 one. Browsers cannot tell these apart, which is why both are listed.

Installing on macOS

Open the .dmg and drag VoidCode to Applications. The app is not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so the first launch is refused: open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about VoidCode and choose Open Anyway. (Control-clicking the app and choosing Open no longer works on macOS 15 and later.) If macOS calls the app damaged, clearing the quarantine flag is the equivalent step from a terminal: 

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VoidCode.app

Installing on Windows

Run the .exe. The installer is not signed, so SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC": choose More info, then Run anyway. Nothing else is needed — the app carries its own Python runtime.

Checking what you downloaded

Each file's SHA-256 is listed above. Compare it with the file you received: 

macOS:    shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/VoidCode-*.dmg
Windows:  Get-FileHash $HOME\Downloads\VoidCode-*.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Linux builds (AppImage and .deb) and older versions are published alongside these files.
